## 🔥 Anti-Inflammatory
**Inflammation** = your body's defense response when it detects damage or threat. It causes redness, swelling, heat, and pain.
- Think of it like your body sending an **army of soldiers** to fight a problem
- Short-term inflammation is good (heals wounds, fights infection)
- Long-term/chronic inflammation is bad (damages healthy cells, causes disease)
**Anti-inflammatory** = anything that **reduces or suppresses** this response
|Type|Examples|
|---|---|
|Foods|Turmeric (haldi), ginger, fish, berries, green tea|
|Medicines|Ibuprofen, aspirin, steroids|
|Skincare|Aloe vera, niacinamide, centella asiatica|
|Lifestyle|Exercise, good sleep, stress reduction|
---
## ⚡ Anti-Oxidant
**Oxidation** = a chemical reaction inside your body where unstable molecules called **free radicals** damage healthy cells
- Think of it like **rust forming on iron** — free radicals "rust" your cells
- Caused by pollution, UV sun exposure, junk food, smoking, stress
- Leads to aging, cancer, heart disease over time
**Anti-oxidant** = molecules that **neutralize free radicals** before they cause damage
|Type|Examples|
|---|---|
|Foods|Amla (highest antioxidant in India), pomegranate, spinach, green tea|
|Vitamins|Vitamin C, Vitamin E|
|Skincare|Vitamin C serum, retinol, green tea extract|
|Minerals|Zinc, selenium|
---
## Key difference (simple):
||Anti-Inflammatory|Anti-Oxidant|
|---|---|---|
|**Fights**|Swelling/redness/immune overreaction|Cell damage from free radicals|
|**Analogy**|Calms down an overreacting army|Stops rust from forming|
|**Overlap**|Many foods/ingredients are both|Many foods/ingredients are both|
---
**India note:** **Turmeric (haldi)** is both anti-inflammatory AND anti-oxidant — which is why it's been used in Indian cooking and Ayurveda for centuries. Modern science has confirmed this. It's one of the most powerful natural sources of both properties.
