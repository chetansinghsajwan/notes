**Sebum**
- Natural oily substance produced by **sebaceous glands** in the skin.
- Made mainly of lipids (fats).
**What it does**
- Keeps skin and hair **moisturized**.
- Forms a **protective barrier** against dryness and microbes.
- Maintains **skin flexibility**.
**Too much sebum**
- Oily skin
- Clogged pores
- Acne
**Too little sebum**
- Dry, flaky skin
- Irritation
- Weak skin barrier
