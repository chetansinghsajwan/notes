Urea is a **natural waste compound** that your body actually produces itself — found in urine, sweat, and skin naturally.
---
## In your body:
- When your body breaks down **protein**, it produces ammonia as a byproduct
- Ammonia is toxic, so your liver converts it into the safer compound — **urea**
- Urea is then excreted through urine and sweat
- Your skin naturally contains small amounts of urea as part of its **Natural Moisturizing Factor (NMF)**
---
## Why urea is in skincare:
Your skin's natural urea does 3 jobs:
|Job|What it does|
|---|---|
|**Humectant**|Attracts and holds water molecules in skin|
|**Keratolytic**|Breaks down and softens hardened keratin|
|**Penetration enhancer**|Helps other ingredients absorb deeper into skin|
Since KP is literally a **keratin overproduction problem**, urea's keratolytic property directly attacks the root issue.
---
## How urea concentration changes its job:
|Concentration|Primary effect|Best for|

|10%|Moisturizing + mild exfoliation|KP, rough skin|

|25–40%|Heavy keratin breakdown|Cracked heels, thick calluses|
|40%+|Prescription grade|Medical conditions only|
---
## Is it safe to put urea on skin?
Yes — completely safe because:
- It is **identical** to what your skin naturally produces
- Synthetic urea in skincare is lab-made but chemically the same
- No risk of irritation at 10–20% concentrations for most people
- Been used in dermatology for **over 50 years**
---
## Urea vs Lactic Acid for KP — which is better?
|Factor|Urea|Lactic Acid|
|---|---|---|
|Keratin breakdown|✅ Strong|✅ Good|
|Moisturizing|✅ Very strong|✅ Good|
|Skin brightening|❌ No|✅ Yes|
|Reduces PIH/dark spots|❌ No|✅ Yes|
|Best concentration for KP|20%|10–12%|
|Smell|Slight (fades quickly)|None|
**Verdict:** Both work well — **urea** is better for pure texture/bump reduction, **lactic acid** is better if you also have dark spots from KP on Indian skin.
