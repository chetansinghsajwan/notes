The primary differences between single-blade and multi-blade razors depend on your skin type, hair texture, and budget:
# **Core Differences**

# **Indian Climate & Skin Impact**
In hot, humid regions across India, sweating opens pores and makes the skin more vulnerable to bacterial acne and folliculitis (inflammation of hair follicles).
- **Multi-blade razors** can aggravate this by cutting curly or thick Indian facial hair beneath the skin level, leading to painful ingrown hairs as the follicle grows back through sweat-laden pores.
- **Single-blade razors** are highly recommended by dermatologists for sensitive, acne-prone skin because the blades are cheap enough to be replaced after every 2 to 3 shaves, ensuring optimal hygiene.
