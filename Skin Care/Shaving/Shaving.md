**Body hair shaving – practical guidelines**
- **Trim first**
	- If hair is long, trim with clippers to avoid razor clogging and tugging.
- **Warm prep**
	- Shower or use warm water for 5–10 minutes to soften hair and open pores.
- **Use lubrication**
	- Shaving gel/cream or a gentle conditioner reduces friction.
	- Avoid dry shaving.
- **Razor choice**
	- Clean, sharp razor.
	- Fewer blades = less irritation for sensitive skin.
- **Direction**
	- First pass **with the grain** (hair growth).
	- Against the grain only if needed and skin tolerates it.
- **Pressure & strokes**
	- Light pressure.
	- Short strokes; rinse blade often.
- **Rinse & soothe**
	- Rinse with cool water to calm skin.
	- Pat dry (don’t rub).
- **Post-shave care**
	- Apply fragrance-free moisturizer or aloe.
	- Avoid alcohol-based aftershaves on body.
- **Frequency**
	- Every 3–7 days depending on growth and irritation.
- **Avoid immediately after**
	- Heavy sweating, tight clothes, sun exposure for ~24 hours.
- **Common mistakes**
	- Old blades, dry skin, rushing, re-shaving the same spot repeatedly.

# Shaving Cream
Shaving cream serves three primary functions to ensure a safe and smooth shave:
- **Lubrication:** It creates a thin, protective layer between the blade and your skin. This minimizes friction, reduces the risk of nicks and cuts, and helps prevent razor burn.
- **Hydration:** It softens the facial hair by locking in moisture. Softened hair requires less force to cut, making the shaving stroke much smoother.
- **Desensitization and Tracking:** The lather lifts the hairs away from the skin for a cleaner cut. It also acts as a visual guide, showing you exactly where the razor has already passed so you do not over-shave and irritate the same spot.
