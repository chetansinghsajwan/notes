An itch (called **pruritus**) is a sensation arising from irritation of skin cells or nerve endings. While annoying, it's actually a **protective mechanism** — it alerts you to harmful things on your skin.
---
**How it works (step by step):**
- Something triggers your skin (chemical, touch, heat, bacteria)
- Skin releases **histamine** — the central chemical of itching — from mast cells
- Histamine binds to nerve endings called **pruriceptors**
- Specialized cells in your spine act as a gateway, deciding whether to send the itch signal to your brain
- Brain receives signal → you feel the urge to scratch
---
**Common triggers of itching:**
|Trigger|Example|
|---|---|
|Dry skin|Most common in India during AC/winter|
|Allergic reaction|Dust, pollen, food, soap|
|Insect bite|Mosquito releases histamine into skin|
|Skin conditions|Eczema, KP, psoriasis|
|Bacteria on skin|A common skin bacterium called _Staphylococcus aureus_ can directly trigger itch by acting on nerve cells|
|Internal conditions|Liver disease, diabetes, kidney issues|
|Psychological|Stress and anxiety can cause itching|
---
**Why scratching feels good (but makes it worse):**
- Scratching interferes with itch signals by stimulating pain and touch receptors in the same area — giving temporary relief
- But it only lasts briefly and can tear skin, cause infection, and worsen inflammation — called the **itch-scratch cycle**
---
**When to be concerned:**
- Itching all over body with no rash → could signal liver, kidney, or thyroid issues
- If itching persists for **6 weeks or more**, it's called **chronic pruritus** and needs a doctor's evaluation
- Itching after warm shower specifically → can be a sign of a blood condition called polycythemia
---
**India note:** The most common cause of itching in India is plain **dry skin + heat + sweat + dust** combination — especially in Chennai's humidity. A good unscented moisturizer (₹200–₹500) used daily fixes most routine itching.
