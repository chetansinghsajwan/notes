#### ==Ingredients to look for==
|Priority|Ingredient|What it does|Skin type|
|---|---|---|---|
|1st|**Ceramides**|Repairs and strengthens skin barrier|All skin types|
|2nd|**Hyaluronic Acid**|Deep hydration, pulls water into skin|All skin types|
|3rd|**Glycerin**|Locks moisture in, prevents water loss|All skin types|
|4th|**Niacinamide**|Reduces dark spots, controls oil, strengthens barrier|Oily, combination, pigmented skin|
|5th|**Urea (10–20%)**|Softens rough skin, breaks down dead skin cells|Dry, rough, KP skin|
|6th|**Lactic Acid (5–12%)**|Exfoliates + hydrates simultaneously|Dry, rough, dull skin|
|7th|**Squalane**|Lightweight oil, non-greasy, non-comedogenic|All skin types|
|8th|**Shea Butter**|Heavy duty moisturization|Very dry, cracked skin|
|9th|**Aloe Vera**|Soothing, cooling, lightweight|Sensitive, oily, acne-prone|
|10th|**Vitamin E**|Antioxidant, repairs damage|Dry, damaged skin|
|11th|**Peptides**|Anti-aging, firms skin, repairs|Aging, mature skin|
|12th|**Centella Asiatica**|Calming, anti-inflammatory, healing|Sensitive, acne-prone skin|
#### ==Ingredients to avoid==
|Priority|Ingredient|Why avoid|
|---|---|---|
|1st|**Fragrance/Parfum**|#1 cause of skin irritation and allergies|
|2nd|**Denatured Alcohol**|Dries skin out, damages barrier long term|
|3rd|**Mineral Oil**|Clogs pores, suffocates skin|
|4th|**Parabens**|Controversial preservative, hormone disruption concerns|
|5th|**Sulfates**|Strips natural oils aggressively|
|6th|**Formaldehyde**|Toxic preservative, causes irritation|
|7th|**Synthetic dyes**|No benefit, potential irritant|
#### ==By skin type — what to prioritize==
|Skin Type|Must have|Avoid|
|---|---|---|
|**Oily**|Hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, aloe vera|Shea butter, heavy oils|
|**Dry**|Ceramides, shea butter, glycerin, urea|Alcohol, fragrance|
|**Combination**|Hyaluronic acid, ceramides, niacinamide|Heavy creams, mineral oil|
|**Sensitive**|Ceramides, centella asiatica, aloe vera|Fragrance, alcohol, acids|
|**Acne-prone**|Niacinamide, squalane, aloe vera|Mineral oil, heavy butters|
|**Aging/mature**|Peptides, ceramides, hyaluronic acid|Alcohol, fragrance|
|**KP/rough**|Urea 20%, lactic acid, ceramides|Mineral oil, fragrance|
|**Pigmented/dark spots**|Niacinamide, lactic acid, vitamin C|Fragrance, alcohol|
#### ==Format to pick based on skin type==
|Format|Best for|Texture|
|---|---|---|
|**Gel**|Oily, acne-prone|Lightest|
|**Lotion**|Normal, combination|Light-medium|
|**Cream**|Dry, KP, rough skin|Medium-heavy|
|**Ointment/Balm**|Very dry, cracked skin|Heaviest|
#### ==India, Chennai specific==
- Chennai's humidity means **gel or lotion formats** feel most comfortable year round
- Heavy creams feel sticky and uncomfortable in humid weather — save for AC environments or night use
- **Fragrance-free is especially important** in Indian heat — fragranced moisturizers + sweat = skin irritation
- Most Indians have **combination to oily skin** — hyaluronic acid + niacinamide combination works best for majority
