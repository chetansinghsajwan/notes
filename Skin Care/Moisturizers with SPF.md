Should I get moisturizer with SPF or separate moisturizer and sunscreen?
Short answer: **Separate sunscreen always wins**
#### ==Why moisturizer with SPF is not enough:==
|Problem|Detail|
|---|---|
|**Insufficient quantity**|You need **2mg per cm²** of sunscreen for rated SPF — nobody applies that much moisturizer|
|**Diluted SPF**|SPF in moisturizer is mixed with moisturizing ingredients — reduces effectiveness|
|**Uneven coverage**|Moisturizer is rubbed in thoroughly — breaks up the UV-blocking film|
|**Lower actual SPF**|SPF 30 moisturizer realistically gives you **SPF 5–10** protection in practice|
|**Reapplication**|You don't reapply moisturizer every 2 hours — but sunscreen needs reapplication|
#### ==Direct comparison:==
|Factor|Moisturizer with SPF|Separate Sunscreen|
|---|---|---|
|**Actual UV protection**|Low — SPF heavily diluted|Full rated SPF protection|
|**Coverage**|Uneven|Even film|
|**Reapplication**|Rarely done|Done every 2 hours|
|**UVA protection**|Usually poor|PA++++ available|
|**Convenience**|High|Moderate|
|**Cost**|One product|Two products|
|**Good enough for indoors?**|Yes|Yes|
|**Good enough for outdoors?**|❌ No|✅ Yes|
#### ==When moisturizer with SPF is acceptable:==
- Spending **entire day indoors**
- Cloudy day with **zero sun exposure**
- Quick errand under **5 minutes** outdoors
#### ==Correct order of application:==
|Step|Product|Wait time|
|---|---|---|
|1|Moisturizer (CeraVe/Dermadew)|2–3 mins to absorb|
|2|Separate sunscreen SPF 50 PA++++ on top|Last step before going out|
