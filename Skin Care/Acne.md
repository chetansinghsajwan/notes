- A **skin condition** where hair follicles (pores) get **blocked by oil (sebum) and dead skin cells**.
- Leads to **pimples, blackheads, whiteheads**, sometimes **painful cysts or nodules**.
- Common on **face, chest, back, shoulders**.
**Why it happens**:
- Excess **sebum production**
- **Clogged pores**
- Growth of **Cutibacterium acnes** bacteria
- **Inflammation**
- Hormonal changes (puberty, stress, hormones)
**Notable facts**:
- Not caused by dirt.
- Can affect **teenagers and adults**.
- Can leave **scars or dark spots** if severe or picked.
