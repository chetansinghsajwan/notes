**SPF = Sun Protection Factor** — measures how well a sunscreen protects against **UVB rays** (the rays that cause sunburn and skin cancer)
---
## How SPF numbers work:
|SPF|% UVB blocked|UVB that gets through|
|---|---|---|
|SPF 15|93%|7%|
|SPF 30|97%|3%|
|SPF 40|97.5%|2.5%|
|SPF 50|98%|2%|
|SPF 100|99%|1%|
**Key insight:** The jump from SPF 30 to SPF 50 is tiny (just 1% more protection). Beyond SPF 50, the difference is negligible.
---
## SPF 40+ simply means:
- SPF of **40 or higher**
- Blocks **97.5% or more** of UVB rays
- The "+" just means it could be 40, 50, 60, or higher
---
## UVA vs UVB — what SPF doesn't tell you:
||UVA|UVB|
|---|---|---|
|What it causes|Aging, dark spots, tanning|Sunburn, skin cancer|
|Penetrates|Deep into skin|Surface of skin|
|SPF protects against?|❌ No|✅ Yes|
|What protects against it?|PA rating / broad spectrum label|SPF number|
---
## PA Rating (important for India):
Since SPF only covers UVB, look for **PA rating** for UVA protection:
|PA Rating|UVA Protection|
|---|---|
|PA+|Some|
|PA++|Moderate|
|PA+++|High|
|PA++++|Highest|
**Ideal sunscreen:** SPF 40–50 + PA+++ or PA++++
---
## 🇮🇳 India-specific notes:
- India has **intense UV radiation year-round**, especially in Chennai — SPF 30 minimum, SPF 50 recommended daily
- Reapply every **2 hours** if outdoors — SPF doesn't last all day
- If you use lactic acid or glycolic acid (for KP), **sunscreen is mandatory** — those ingredients make skin more sun-sensitive
