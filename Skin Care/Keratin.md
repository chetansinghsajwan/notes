A **structural protein** — one of the most important building blocks your body produces. It forms the physical structure of several body parts.
---
## Where keratin exists in your body:
|Body part|Role of keratin|
|---|---|
|**Skin**|Forms protective outer layer|
|**Hair**|Makes up 95% of hair structure|
|**Nails**|Makes nails hard and strong|
|**Teeth enamel**|Partial component|
|**Hooves/horns** (animals)|Primary structural component|
---
## What keratin actually does:
|Function|Detail|
|---|---|
|**Protection**|Acts as physical barrier against damage, bacteria, UV|
|**Waterproofing**|Prevents water loss from skin|
|**Structural strength**|Keeps hair/nails strong and rigid|
|**Wound healing**|Helps skin repair itself after injury|
---
## How keratin is made:
- Specialized skin cells called **keratinocytes** produce keratin
- These cells start deep in skin, produce keratin, then slowly move to surface
- By the time they reach the surface they are dead — forming the tough outer skin layer
- Old keratin sheds off → new keratin replaces it — cycle repeats every **~28 days**
---
## Types of keratin:
|Type|Found in|Property|
|---|---|---|
|**Alpha keratin**|Skin, hair, nails|Softer, flexible|
|**Beta keratin**|Bird feathers, reptile scales|Harder, rigid|
Humans only have **alpha keratin**.
---
## Keratin — good vs bad:
|Situation|What happens|
|---|---|
|**Normal keratin production**|Smooth skin, healthy hair, strong nails|
|**Too little keratin**|Weak brittle nails, fragile hair, skin damage|
|**Too much keratin (KP)**|Clogs hair follicles → bumps on skin|
|**Keratin buildup on scalp**|Dandruff|
|**Keratin buildup on feet**|Calluses, cracked heels|
---
## Why keratin is relevant to your KP:
- Your DNA instructs keratinocytes to **overproduce keratin**
- Excess keratin has nowhere to go → gets trapped inside hair follicles
- Forms hard **keratin plugs** → pushes up through skin → forms the bumps you see on your arms and legs
- Urea and lactic acid work by **dissolving these keratin plugs** — which is why they're the best treatments for KP

## Keratin in haircare (you may have heard of this):
|Product|What it claims|Reality|
|---|---|---|
|Keratin hair treatment (Brazilian blowout)|Smooths frizzy hair by adding keratin|Works temporarily — keratin coats hair shaft externally|
