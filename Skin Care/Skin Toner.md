A facial toner is a liquid skincare product applied after cleansing and before moisturizing. It helps prep the skin for better absorption of other products while offering specific skin benefits based on its ingredients. 
  
The Main Purpose of Toner Includes:
- Balancing the skin's pH levels
- Hydrating and refreshing the skin
- Minimizing pores
- Removing residual dirt, oil, or makeup
- Enhancing product penetration
  
==Toner Ingredients You Should Know==
Hydrating Agents
- Hyaluronic Acid: Binds water molecules to keep skin hydrated.
- Glycerin: A classic humectant that draws moisture to the skin.
Astringents & Pore Minimizers
- Witch Hazel: Tightens skin and reduces inflammation.
- Tea Tree Oil: Natural antibacterial agent ideal for breakouts.
Exfoliants
- Salicylic Acid: Clears clogged pores and reduces acne.
- Glycolic Acid: Promotes cell turnover.
- Lactic Acid: Mild exfoliant with added moisturizing benefits.
Antioxidants
- Vitamin C: Brightens the skin and boosts collagen.
- Green Tea Extract: Protects against environmental stressors.
- Niacinamide: Improves texture and reduces fine lines.
==How to Use a Toner Correctly==
1. Cleanse Your Face thoroughly to remove all dirt and makeup.
2. Apply 3-4 Drops of toner into your palm or on a cotton pad.
3. Gently Pat or Swipe the toner over your face, avoiding the eye and lip area.
4. Let It Absorb for a few seconds before moving to the next step.
5. Follow With Serum or Moisturizer to lock in hydration.
Pro Tip: Use toner morning and evening for best results, especially if it's hydrating or calming.
==Myth: All toners contain alcohol==
Many modern toners are alcohol-free and focus on hydration and calming.
==Myth: Toners make skin dry== 
Only some astringent toners can be drying. Hydrating toners actually help with moisture retention.
==Myth: Toner is unnecessary== 
While not essential for everyone, toner can significantly improve the effectiveness of your skincare routine.
