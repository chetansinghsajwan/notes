==Based on oil production==
- **Normal skin**
	- Balanced oil + moisture
	- Few breakouts, even texture
- **Oily skin**
	- Excess sebum
	- Shiny look, acne-prone
- **Dry skin**
	- Low sebum
	- Flaky, tight, rough
- **Combination skin**
	- Oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin)
	- Dry or normal cheeks
==Based on sensitivity==
**Sensitive skin**
- Reacts easily (redness, itching, burning)
- Triggered by products, weather, pollution
==Factors affecting skin type==
- **Genetics**
	- Controls sebaceous gland size & activity
	- Biggest, permanent factor
- **Hormones**
	- Androgens ↑ sebum
	- Puberty, periods, pregnancy, PCOS matter
- **Age**
	- Sebum peaks in teens–20s
	- Declines with age → skin gets drier
- **Climate**
	- Hot/humid → oilier
	- Cold/dry → drier
- **Ethnicity**
	- Average sebum output differs across populations
- **Skincare habits**
	- Over-washing → rebound oil
	- Heavy products → pore clogging
- **Medications**
	- Steroids, isotretinoin, hormonal meds
- **Diet**
	- High glycemic load may increase acne/oil (evidence moderate)
==What does not truly change skin type==
- Pollution
- Tanning
- Drinking more water
- Short-term product use
==Key takeaway==
- **Skin type = genetics + hormones**
- Everything else just nudges it temporarily
==Identify skin type==
==The **wash test** (gold standard at home)==
- Wash face with a **gentle cleanser**
- Don’t apply anything
- Wait **60–90 minutes**
**Observe**
- Tight, flaky → **Dry**
- Comfortable, no shine → **Normal**
- Shiny everywhere → **Oily**
- Shiny T-zone, dry cheeks → **Combination**
==The **blotting paper test** (confirmation)==
- Press blotting paper on:
	- Forehead
	- Nose
	- Chin
	- Cheeks
**Result**
- Little/no oil → **Dry**
- Even light oil → **Normal**
- Heavy oil everywhere → **Oily**
- Oil only on T-zone → **Combination**
==What to ignore==
- Mid-day shine (can be sweat)
- Acne alone
- Seasonal changes
- Product reactions
==Best conditions for accuracy==
- Test in the **morning**
- Avoid actives (retinoids, acids) 24 hrs before
- Do it over **3–5 days**, not once
