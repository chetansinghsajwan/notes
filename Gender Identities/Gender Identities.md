---
Binary Gender Terms
|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|**Cisgender (Cis)**|Person whose gender identity matches their birth sex|
|**Transgender (Trans)**|Person whose gender identity differs from their birth sex|
|**Male**|Birth sex with XY chromosomes and male anatomy|
|**Female**|Birth sex with XX chromosomes and female anatomy|
---
### Transgender Specific Terms
|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|**MTF / Trans Woman**|Person assigned male at birth, identifies as woman|
|**FTM / Trans Man**|Person assigned female at birth, identifies as man|
|**Non-binary (NB)**|Person who doesn't identify exclusively as man or woman|
|**Genderqueer**|Umbrella term for non-binary gender identities|
|**Genderfluid**|Person whose gender identity shifts over time|
|**Agender**|Person who identifies as having no gender|
|**Bigender**|Person who identifies as two genders simultaneously|
|**Pangender**|Person who identifies with all genders|
|**Demi-gender**|Partially identifies with one gender but not fully|
|**Neutrois**|Person who identifies as neutral or null gender|
|**Transmasculine**|Trans person moving toward masculine identity|
|**Transfeminine**|Trans person moving toward feminine identity|
---
### South Asian / Indian Specific Terms
|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|**Hijra**|Ancient South Asian identity — assigned male at birth, feminine identity; recognized third gender in India, Pakistan, Bangladesh|
|**Kinnar**|Hindi/North Indian term for Hijra community members|
|**Aravani / Thirunangai**|Tamil Nadu term for trans women — preferred over Hijra in Tamil culture|
|**Shiv-Shakti**|Andhra Pradesh/Telangana term for assigned male, feminine identity|
|**Jogta/Jogappa**|Karnataka/Maharashtra term — devotees of goddess Yellamma, often assigned male with feminine identity|
|**Kothi**|Feminine-presenting person assigned male at birth — distinct from Hijra, usually not castrated|
|**Panthi**|Masculine partner of a Kothi|
|**Dupli/Giriya**|Person who can be both masculine and feminine|

|Term|Definition|
|---|---|

|**Lesbian**|Woman attracted to women|
|**Bisexual (Bi)**|Attracted to both men and women|
|**Pansexual (Pan)**|Attracted to people regardless of gender|
|**Asexual (Ace)**|Little or no sexual attraction to anyone|
|**Demisexual**|Sexual attraction only after emotional bond forms|
|**Heterosexual/Straight**|Attracted to opposite gender|
|**Queer**|Umbrella term for non-heterosexual/non-cisgender identities|
|**Aromantic**|Little or no romantic attraction to anyone|

### Biological/Medical Terms
|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|**Intersex**|Person born with sex characteristics that don't fit typical male/female definitions — chromosomes, hormones, or anatomy|
|**DSD**|Differences of Sex Development — medical term for intersex conditions|
|**Dyadic**|Person who is not intersex — standard male or female biology|
|**AMAB**|Assigned Male At Birth|
|**AFAB**|Assigned Female At Birth|
|**Sex**|Biological — chromosomes, hormones, anatomy|
|**Gender**|Social/psychological — identity and expression|
---
### Gender Expression Terms
|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|**Masculine**|Expression aligned with traditional male presentation|
|**Feminine**|Expression aligned with traditional female presentation|
|**Androgynous**|Expression blending masculine and feminine|
|**Gender non-conforming (GNC)**|Expression that doesn't match gender norms|
|**Cross-dresser**|Person who wears clothing of another gender — not necessarily trans|
|**Drag Queen**|Usually male performer in exaggerated feminine presentation|
|**Drag King**|Usually female performer in exaggerated masculine presentation|
|**Femboy**|Masculine-identified person with feminine expression|
|**Butch**|Masculine-presenting lesbian or woman|
|**Femme**|Feminine-presenting lesbian or queer woman|
---
### Transition Related Terms
|Term|Definition|
|---|---|
|**Gender Dysphoria**|Distress from mismatch between gender identity and birth sex|
|**Gender Euphoria**|Joy from alignment between gender identity and expression|
|**Transitioning**|Process of aligning life/body with gender identity|
|**Social transition**|Changing name, pronouns, clothing, presentation|
|**Medical transition**|HRT, surgeries to align body with gender identity|
|**Legal transition**|Changing name/gender on official documents|
|**Passing**|Being perceived as your identified gender by others|
|**Stealth**|Living as identified gender without disclosing trans history|
|**Out**|Openly identifying as LGBTQ+|
|**Closeted**|Not publicly disclosing LGBTQ+ identity|

### India Legal Recognition 🇮🇳
|Identity|Legal Status in India|
|---|---|
|**Male**|✅ Recognized|
|**Female**|✅ Recognized|
|**Transgender/Third Gender**|✅ Recognized — Transgender Persons Act 2019|
|**Hijra**|✅ Recognized under third gender|
|**Intersex**|⚠️ Limited legal protection|
---

> In simple terms: **Gender is a vast spectrum — from biological sex to identity to expression to culture. Indian culture has its own ancient gender diverse identities like Hijra and Aravani that predate Western terminology by centuries. Sexual orientation (who you're attracted to) is completely separate from gender identity (who you are).**
