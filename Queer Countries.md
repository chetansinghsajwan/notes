### Key Notes by Identity
- Iceland and Malta have retained their leadership on trans rights — both recognize non-binary identities, prohibit conversion therapy, and offer legal gender recognition with minimal barriers.
- Legal gender recognition based on self-determination exists in Belgium, Denmark, Finland, Germany, Iceland, Ireland, Luxembourg, Malta, Norway, Portugal, Spain and Switzerland. Non-binary gender is fully recognized only in Germany, Iceland, and Malta.
- Nepal, Thailand, Israel, Taiwan, and India stand out as the most LGBT+ friendly countries in Asia according to the Equaldex index in 2025.

### India-Specific Immigration Tips 🇮🇳
- **Canada** — widely considered the easiest country to immigrate from India due to its transparent Express Entry system, high PR approval rates, and a range of programs for skilled workers, students, and families
- **Portugal** — growing Indian community, D7 passive income visa very accessible, among the most affordable EU countries
- **Germany** — new Opportunity Card allows Indians to job search while in Germany without a job offer first
- **Australia** — points-based system favors Indian IT professionals, very large Indian community
- **Thailand** — easiest for short-term relocation, very trans-accepting socially, very affordable, but limited long-term legal rights for queer people
