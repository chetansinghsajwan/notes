# Features
- **Dual visor**
    
	• Clear outer visor + built-in inner tinted visor (sun protection)
    
	• Inner visor replaces the need for a separate “sun visor” feature
    
- **Shell**
    
	• Outer impact-resistant layer (ABS / fiberglass / carbon, etc.)
    
	• Distributes crash force and protects inner EPS liner
    
- **Pinlock**
    
	• Anti-fog insert for the main visor
    
	• Prevents fogging in rain, cold, and humidity
    
- **Double D-ring safety lock**
    
	• Metal ring chin-strap system
    
	• Most secure retention, used in racing and track riding
    
- **EPS liner (impact foam)**
    
	• Inner energy-absorbing foam
    
	• Real crash protection happens here, not in the shell
    
- **Ventilation system**
    
	• Intake + exhaust vents for airflow
    
	• Reduces heat, sweat, and visor fog
    
- **Visor material & coating**
    
	• Polycarbonate visor
    
	• Scratch-resistant + UV-blocking coatings
    
- **Visor quick-release mechanism**
    
	• Tool-less visor removal
    
	• Useful for cleaning or swapping visors
    
- **Chin curtain**
    
	• Reduces wind noise and cold air
    
	• Improves comfort at highway speeds
    
- **Neck roll**
    
	• Cushioned lower padding
    
	• Improves fit, noise isolation, and stability
    
- **Interior padding (liner)**
    
	• Removable & washable
    
	• Sweat-wicking, antibacterial fabric
    
- **Speaker cut-outs**
    
	• Space for Bluetooth intercom speakers
    
	• Prevents ear pressure
    
- **Weight**
    
	• Typically 1.4–1.7 kg for full-face helmets
    
	• Lighter = less neck fatigue on long rides
    
- **Shell size options**
    
	• Multiple shell sizes for same helmet model
    
	• Better proportions and safety than single-shell helmets
    
- **Aerodynamics / spoiler**
    
	• Reduces lift and buffeting at high speeds
    
	• More relevant for touring and sport riding
    
- **Emergency cheek pad release**
    
	• Allows safe helmet removal after a crash
    
	• Found in higher-end helmets
    
- **Breath guard / nose deflector**
    
	• Redirects breath downward
    
	• Reduces visor fogging
    
- **Bluetooth readiness**
    
	• Pre-cut slots and wiring channels
    
	• Cleaner install for intercom systems
