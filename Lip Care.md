**Your lips have NO natural sun protection at all.**
---
## Why lips are extra vulnerable:
|Factor|Detail|
|---|---|
|**No melanin**|Lips have little to no melanin (the pigment that naturally protects skin from UV)|
|**Very thin skin**|Lip skin is much thinner than face skin — UV penetrates easily|
|**No hair follicles**|No follicles = no sebum = no natural moisture barrier|
|**Constantly exposed**|Lips face direct sun all day with zero cover|
|**Frequently licked**|Saliva removes any applied protection quickly|
---
## What UV damage does to lips:
|Damage|Detail|
|---|---|
|Darkening/pigmentation|Very common in Indians — lips turn darker over time|
|Dryness & chapping|UV breaks down moisture barrier|
|Premature aging|Lips lose volume and definition faster|
|Actinic cheilitis|Chronic sun damage to lips — precancerous condition|
|Lip cancer|Lower lip especially — one of the more common oral cancers|
---
## 🇮🇳 India-specific note:
- Lip darkening is **extremely common** among Indians — most people blame genetics but UV exposure + licking lips is usually the main cause
- Chennai's year-round strong sun makes this worse
- A simple SPF lip balm fixes most of this
---
## What to look for in a lip moisturizer:
|Ingredient|Why|
|---|---|
|**SPF 30+**|UV protection|
|**Shea butter / cocoa butter**|Deep moisture|
|**Vitamin E**|Antioxidant, repairs damage|
|**Castor oil**|Seals moisture in|
|**Avoid: Camphor, menthol**|Feel nice but dry lips out long term|
