# Profiles
**PlayStation profiles (user profiles)** = separate user accounts on the same PlayStation console.
**What they are**
- Individual user spaces on one console.
- Each profile has its own settings, saves, and identity.
**What a profile includes**
- Saved games and trophies (separate per user)
- Controller & system preferences
- Privacy and parental controls
- Friends list and messages (if signed in online)
**Local vs online**
- **Local profile**: Offline-only, no friends, no cloud saves.
- **PSN profile**: Linked to a PlayStation Network account → online play, store access, cloud saves, trophies sync.
**Why they exist**
- Multiple people sharing one console
- Keep progress and settings separate
- Parental control for kids’ accounts
**Important notes**
- Games installed once can be played by all profiles (with limits).
- Online play usually requires PS Plus on the profile (or shared via primary console).

# Playstation Plus
**PlayStation Plus (PS Plus)** = a paid subscription that unlocks online features and extra content on PlayStation.
**What you get**
- Online multiplayer for paid games
- Monthly free games (play while subscribed)
- Cloud saves (game data backed up online)
- Exclusive discounts on PlayStation Store
**Plans (India pricing, approx.)**
- **Essential** – Online play + monthly games
    
	₹749/month • ₹1,999/3 months • ₹4,999/year
    
- **Extra** – Essential + game catalog (hundreds of PS4/PS5 games)
    
	₹1,099/month • ₹2,999/3 months • ₹7,499/year
    
- **Deluxe** _(India)_ – Extra + classics + trials
    
	₹1,199/month • ₹3,199/3 months • ₹7,599/year
    
	_(Premium streaming not available in India)_
    
**Important notes**
- Free-to-play games (Fortnite, Warzone, Apex) do **not** need PS Plus for online.
- Subscription is per account, but benefits can be shared on the primary console.

# PSN
**PlayStation Network (PSN)** = Sony’s online service that connects your PlayStation to the internet ecosystem.
**What PSN is**
- A free account-based network.
- Required to use online features on PlayStation.
**What you can do with PSN**
- Create an online PlayStation account (PSN ID).
- Buy & download games from PlayStation Store.
- Earn and sync trophies.
- Add friends, chat, join parties.
- Back up saves to cloud _(needs PS Plus)_.
- Play online multiplayer _(needs PS Plus for paid games)_.
**PSN vs PS Plus**
- **PSN**: Free account + network (basic access).
- **PS Plus**: Paid subscription that adds online multiplayer, free games, cloud saves, etc.
**Important notes**
- One PSN account can be used on multiple consoles.
- Your purchases are tied to your PSN account, not the console.
- Region of PSN account affects store content & pricing.
