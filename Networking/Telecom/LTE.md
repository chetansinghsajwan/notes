It stands for Long Term Evolution.
It is a standard for [wireless broadband](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Wireless_broadband) communication for [mobile devices](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Mobile_device) and data terminals, based on the [GSM](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/GSM)/[EDGE](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Enhanced_Data_Rates_for_GSM_Evolution) and [UMTS](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/UMTS)/[HSPA](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/High_Speed_Packet_Access) standards.
