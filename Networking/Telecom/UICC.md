It stands for Universal Integrated Ciruit Card.
It is the physical card which contains [[SIM]]
The official definition for UICC is found in [ETSI](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/ETSI) TR 102 216, where it is defined as a “[smart card](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Smart_card) that conforms to the specifications written and maintained by the ETSI Smart Card Platform project”.
## Design
A UICC consists of a [CPU](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Central_processing_unit), [ROM](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Read-only_memory), [RAM](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Random_access_memory), [EEPROM](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/EEPROM) and [I/O](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Input/output) circuits.
