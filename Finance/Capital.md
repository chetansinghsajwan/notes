Capital is just **money you use to make more money.**
---
**Everyday analogy 🍋**
Imagine you want to start a lemonade stall:
- You need ₹500 to buy lemons, sugar, cups
- That ₹500 is your **capital**
- You use it to earn ₹1000
- Capital = the starting money that works for you
---
**Types of Capital (Simply)**
|Type|Simple Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Personal Capital|Your own savings|₹50,000 in your bank|
|Debt Capital|Borrowed money|Home loan from SBI|
|Working Capital|Money to run day-to-day business|Cash to pay salaries this month|
|Investment Capital|Money put to work in stocks/MFs|₹5,000 SIP in mutual fund|
---
**Key idea to remember**
- Money sitting idle in a locker = just money
- Money being used to earn more = **capital**
The difference is **intention and action.**
---
**India context 🇮🇳**
- When you do a **SIP in a mutual fund**, that monthly amount is your capital being put to work
- When a business takes a **mudra loan**, that borrowed money is their capital
---
**One line to remember:**

> Capital = Money put to work to earn more money.
