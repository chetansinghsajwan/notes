Androgens are a class of hormones that act as the **primary male sex hormones**.
---
### Key Facts
- Like estrogen, it's a **class** of hormones, not one single hormone
- Found in all humans, but in much higher levels in people with testes
- Controls male sexual development, muscle mass, body hair, and many other functions
---
### The Main Androgens
|Androgen|Also Called|Where Produced|Role|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Testosterone**|T|Testes, adrenal glands|Most abundant; dominant androgen|
|**DHT**|Dihydrotestosterone|Converted from T in tissues|Most potent; drives body/facial hair, prostate|
|**DHEA**|Dehydroepiandrosterone|Adrenal glands|Precursor to T and estrogen|
|**Androstenedione**|A4|Adrenal glands, gonads|Precursor to T and estrogen|
---
### What Androgens Do in the Body
- Development of male genitalia
- Body and facial hair growth
- Deepening of voice
- Muscle mass and strength
- Bone density
- Sperm production
- Libido
- Skin oiliness and acne
---
### Androgens vs Testosterone
||Androgens|Testosterone|
|---|---|---|
|What it is|A hormone **class**|A specific hormone|
|Includes|T, DHT, DHEA, etc.|Just testosterone|
|Blocked in MTF HRT?|All of them (ideally)|Primary target|
