Estradiol is the primary and most potent naturally occurring **estrogen** hormone in the human body.
---
### Key Facts
- Produced mainly in the **ovaries** in cisgender women; also made in small amounts by adrenal glands and fat tissue
- Responsible for development of **female secondary sexual characteristics** (breast development, fat redistribution, skin changes, etc.)
- Plays a role in **bone density, cardiovascular health, and mood**
- In MTF HRT, it's administered externally to feminize the body and suppress testosterone
---
### Estradiol vs Other Estrogens
|Estrogen|Potency|Natural/Synthetic|Used in HRT?|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Estradiol (E2)**|Highest|Natural|✅ Yes (preferred)|
|Estrone (E1)|Medium|Natural|⚠️ Mostly a byproduct|
|Estriol (E3)|Lowest|Natural|Limited|
|Ethinylestradiol|Very high|Synthetic|❌ Avoided (high clot risk)|
