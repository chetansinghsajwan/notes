Rectal means **"via the rectum"** — a method of delivering medication by inserting it into the **back passage (anus)**.
---
### Key Facts
- The drug is absorbed through the **rectal lining** directly into the bloodstream
- Like transdermal, it **bypasses the liver** (avoids first-pass metabolism)
- Gives **higher and more stable** estradiol levels than oral
- Not commonly used but technically very effective
---
### Rectal Forms
|Form|How Used|
|---|---|
|**Suppository**|Solid bullet-shaped insert that melts inside|
|**Enema**|Liquid solution inserted via small tube|
|**Crushed tablet**|Oral estradiol tablet inserted rectally (off-label)|
---
### Rectal vs Other Routes
||Rectal|Oral|Transdermal|
|---|---|---|---|
|Liver bypass|✅ Yes|❌ No|✅ Yes|
|Hormone levels|Stable|Fluctuates|Stable|
|Clot risk|Lower|Higher|Lower|
|Convenience|Low|High|Moderate|
|Comfort|Uncomfortable|Easy|Easy|
|Popularity|Very rare|Common|Common|
---
### Why It's Rarely Used
- Socially stigmatized and uncomfortable
- Less practical for daily use
- Transdermal achieves similar benefits more conveniently
---

> In simple terms: **Medication inserted into the rectum gets absorbed into the blood quickly and efficiently — same benefit as transdermal but far less convenient, which is why it's almost never used in practice.**
