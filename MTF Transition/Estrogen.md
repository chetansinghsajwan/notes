Estrogen is a group of hormones that act as the **primary female sex hormones**.
---
### Key Facts
- It's not one hormone — it's a **class** of hormones
- Found in all humans, but in much higher levels in people with ovaries
- Controls female sexual development, reproduction, and many body functions
---
### The 3 Main Estrogens
|Estrogen|Also Called|Where Produced|Role|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Estradiol**|E2|Ovaries|Most potent; dominant in reproductive years|
|**Estrone**|E1|Fat tissue|Dominant after menopause|
|**Estriol**|E3|Placenta|Dominant during pregnancy|
---
### What Estrogen Does in the Body
- Breast and uterine development
- Fat redistribution (hips, thighs)
- Skin softening
- Bone density maintenance
- Mood and cognition regulation
- Cardiovascular health
- Libido (partially)
---
### Estrogen vs Estradiol
||Estrogen|Estradiol|
|---|---|---|
|What it is|A hormone **class**|A specific hormone|
|Includes|E1, E2, E3 + synthetic forms|Just E2|
|Used in HRT?|Term used loosely|The actual drug prescribed|

> In simple terms: **Estrogen is the category, Estradiol is the specific molecule used in HRT.**
