Progestogens are a class of hormones that act like **progesterone** (the natural female hormone produced after ovulation).
---
### Key Facts
- Like estrogen, it's a **class** of hormones, not one single hormone
- Plays a key role in the **menstrual cycle and pregnancy**
- Used in MTF HRT as an **optional add-on**, usually after 1–2 years of estrogen therapy
- Role in MTF HRT is still **debated** — some report better feminization, others see no difference
---
### Two Types
|Type|Examples|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**Progesterone** (bioidentical)|Micronized progesterone (Utrogestan)|Identical to body's own; preferred|
|**Progestins** (synthetic)|Medroxyprogesterone, Norethisterone, CPA*|Man-made; stronger but more side effects|

> *Cyproterone Acetate (CPA) is technically a progestin **and** an antiandrogen — that's why it's commonly used in MTF HRT in India.
---
### What Progestogens Do
- Support breast development (debated)
- Improve sleep and mood in some people
- Help with bone density
- Can reduce estrogen's effect on the uterine lining (relevant for people with a uterus)
