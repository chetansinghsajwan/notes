Transdermal means **"through the skin"** — a method of delivering medication by absorbing it directly through the skin into the bloodstream.
---
### Key Facts
- From Latin: **trans** (across) + **dermal** (skin)
- The drug bypasses the digestive system and liver entirely
- Absorbed slowly and steadily — gives more **stable hormone levels**
- Generally considered **safer** than oral for long-term HRT use
---
### Transdermal Forms
|Form|How Applied|Frequency|Example Brand (India)|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Patch**|Stick on skin|Twice a week|Climara, Estradot|
|**Gel**|Rub on skin|Daily|Oestrogel, Sandrena|
|**Spray**|Spray on forearm|Daily|Lenzetto|
|**Cream**|Rub on skin|Daily|Less common|
---
### Transdermal vs Oral
||Transdermal|Oral|
|---|---|---|
|Liver involvement|❌ Bypasses liver|✅ Passes through liver|
|Hormone levels|Stable|More fluctuation|
|Clot risk|Lower|Higher|
|Convenience|Moderate|Easy|
|Cost|Higher|Lower|
|India availability|Good|Best|
---
### Where to Apply
- Inner arm, abdomen, thighs, buttocks
- Rotate sites to avoid skin irritation
- Avoid broken or irritated skin
---

> In simple terms: **Instead of swallowing a pill, the medication soaks through your skin directly into your blood — like a nicotine patch but for hormones.**
