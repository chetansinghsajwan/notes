It is a non profit indian [state-owned](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/State-owned) [organization](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Organization) that operates retail payments and settlement systems in India.
It was established by [[Reserve Bank of India|RBI]] and [[Indian Banks Association|IBA]] for creating a robust payment and settlement infrastructure in India.
It was established in december 2008.
It is owned by a consortium of major banks
Initially, there were ten promoter banks viz. [State Bank of India](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/State_Bank_of_India), [Punjab National Bank](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Punjab_National_Bank), [Canara Bank](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Canara_Bank), [Bank of Baroda](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Bank_of_Baroda), [Union Bank of India](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Union_Bank_of_India), [Bank of India](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Bank_of_India), [ICICI Bank](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/ICICI_Bank), [HDFC Bank](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/HDFC_Bank), [Citibank](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Citibank) and [HSBC](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/HSBC).
In 2016, the shareholding was diluted to include 13 additional public sector banks, 15 additional private sector banks, 1 additional foreign bank, 10 multi-state co-operative banks and 7 regional rural banks.
It has two subsidiaries, [[NPCI International Payments Limited|NIPL]] and [[NPCI Bharat Billpay Limited|NBBL]].
The NPCI was awarded Golden Peacock Innovative Product/Service Award for the year 2018 for BHIM UPI.
