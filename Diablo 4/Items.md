==What do you mean by Unique Power?==
Good question — "Unique Power" is just the term I used to describe the special built-in effect that every Unique and Mythic Unique has. It doesn't have one single official name in-game, but here's what it actually means:
**Every Unique item has:**
- A set of **fixed affixes** (the normal stats like crit chance, cooldown reduction, etc.)
- One **special effect** that is exclusive to that item and can't be found anywhere else — this is what I was calling the "Unique Power"
**Example:** The unique boots _Tibault's Will_ have a special effect that gives you bonus damage and Primary Resource when you become Unstoppable. That effect only exists on that specific item — you can't extract it, imprint it, or find it on any other item.
**How it differs from a Legendary Aspect:**
- A Legendary Aspect can be extracted and moved to other items
- A Unique's special effect is **permanently attached** to that item and is **non-transferable**
**Mythic Uniques** follow the same idea but their exclusive effects are significantly more powerful and often interact with multiple systems at once.
So there's no special in-game label for it — it's just the thing that makes each Unique item unique. I probably should have just said "exclusive built-in effect" rather than "Unique Power" to avoid confusion!

### ==Durability==
Durability is purely a **death penalty system** — gear doesn't degrade from normal use
- All items start with 100/100 durability — Amulets and Rings are the only items that have NO durability
==How Durability is Lost==
- Every time you die and respawn yourself, ALL equipped Armor and Weapons lose 10% durability
- There are no penalties until the durability has reached 0
- ~~If someone else resurrects you in co-op, items do NOT lose durability~~ ==- Not sure about this.==
- Hardcore mode — death is permanent, so durability is irrelevant
==What Happens at 0 Durability==
- Item is considered broken — you lose a massive percentage of its stats and bonuses
- You get a Durability Broken status effect above your skill bar — a red broken sword icon
- Item is NOT deleted — still in inventory and can be repaired or salvaged
- Game warns you when durability is dangerously low at the death screen
### ==Transmog==
Transmog is game mechanic which allows you to equip the look of another item of the same category but keep the effect of the currently equipped item.
You can unlock new views by either equipping the item itself or salvaging it.
### ==Account Binding==
It is a system that restricts whether items can be traded with other players
- Prevents real money trading and economy abuse
- Account Bound items cannot be traded to other players but can be shared between characters on the same account via Stash
==Two Types of Binding==
- ==Account Bound -== Locked to your account — can share between your own characters via Stash but cannot trade to other players
- ==Soul bound -== Locked to the specific character that found it — cannot even be shared with your own other characters

==Trading Rare Items==
- Rare items are the **most valuable tradeable items** in the game
- No central Auction House — all trades done manually between players
- Best places to trade: Discord communities like DiabloTrade.gg
==Why Blizzard Does This==
- Prevents real money trading (RMT)
- Prevents third party sites from selling best in slot gear
- Ensures players actually play the game to get powerful items
- Keeps the economy from getting flooded with perfect gear
