**What Are Runes?**
- Socketable items introduced in Season 6 (Vessel of Hatred expansion) that provide powerful bonuses
- **Requires Vessel of Hatred expansion** — base game players cannot use runes
- Runes are tradeable with other players and stored in a dedicated Socketables tab
---
**Two Types of Runes**
**1. Rune of Ritual (Trigger/Generator)**
- Represents an action or condition your character needs to fulfill
- Generates **Offering** when condition is met
- Examples: Move 5 meters, Use a potion, Cast an Ultimate skill, Evade an attack
**2. Rune of Invocation (Effect/Spender)**
- The effect triggered once enough Offering is accumulated
- Examples: Teleport, War Cry, Earthquake, Gain Max Life, Reduce Cooldowns
- Can even give you access to skills from other classes
---
**How Runewords Work**
- Combine one Ritual Rune + one Invocation Rune socketed in the same item to create a Runeword
- Example: Bac (move 5m = 50 Offering) + Jah (500 Offering = Teleport) → move 50m total → next Evade becomes Teleport
- If more Offering is generated than needed, Overflow triggers bonus amplified effects
---
**Runeword Limits**
- Maximum 2 Runewords on your character at any time
- Both runes must be socketed in the same item — valid slots are Helm, Chest, Legs, and 2H Weapon only
- Cannot use same rune twice
- Cannot mix Gems and Runes in the same item — you must choose one or the other
- Cannot use two Ritual or two Invocation runes in same item

|Legendary|Strongest|Rare|

**How to Get Runes**
|Source|Notes|
|---|---|
|World drops|Enemies, chests, events|
|World Bosses|Best drop rate for Legendary runes — guaranteed 3 runes per kill|
|Kurast Undercity|Best consistent farm with Tribute of Harmony active|
|Nightmare Dungeons|Good secondary source|
|Helltide|Treasure Goblins and elite enemies|
|Mercenary Bartering|Buy Rune Caches using Pale Marks|
|Crafting at Jeweler|Combine 3 runes of same name → random rune, 15% chance to upgrade rarity|
|Opal of Socketables|Increases rune drop rate for 30 mins — obtained from Realm Walker events in Nantu|
---
**Crafting Mythic Uniques with Runes**
- Each Mythic Unique requires 2 Resplendent Sparks + 6 Legendary Runes + 6 Rare Runes + 6 Magic Runes of specific names
- Done at the **Occultist**
- This is the only way to target craft Mythic Uniques
---
**Storage & Management**
- Runes stack and are stored in the dedicated Socketables inventory tab
- Unsocketing runes is free and non-destructive — freely swap combinations
- Can be traded with other players — Legendary runes are high value trade currency
---
**Pro Tips**
- Always pair Ritual and Invocation runes that complement your playstyle
- If you equip a rune for your own class skill, it casts the enhanced version based on your skill tree investment
- Save Legendary runes for Mythic Unique crafting or your best in slot items
- Don't put runes in slots where you need gems — plan your socket usage carefully
- Kurast Undercity with Tribute of Harmony is the most efficient Legendary rune farm
