It is an endgame system to further customize and strengthen your character after Level 60.
- Replaces Skill Points — once you hit Level 60 you start earning Paragon Points instead
- Gives massive stat boosts and build-defining bonuses
- Unlocks at level 60
- You get 1 Paragon Point per level after 60
**Paragon Points**
|Source|Points|
|---|---|
|Leveling (1 per level after 60)|Up to 300 points|
|Renown completion per region|4 points per region|
|Altars of Lilith|Bonus points|
|Total Maximum|328 Paragon Points total|
---
**Paragon Points are Realm Wide**
- Paragon Points are shared across ALL your characters on the same realm
- Any character that hits Level 60 instantly has all your accumulated Paragon Points to spend
- Extremely helpful for alt characters
---
**Paragon Boards**
- Each class has 9 boards to choose from — but can only equip 5 including the starting board
- Boards connect through **Board Attachment Gates** at the edges
- You can rotate boards before attaching to optimize your path through nodes
- Cannot reuse the same board twice
- Each board has a theme built around a specific playstyle
**Node Types**
|Node|Color|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|Normal|Grey|+5 to one Attribute (Strength, Intelligence, Willpower, Dexterity)|
|Magic|Blue|Armor, Max Life, Resistances, Resources, Damage bonuses|
|Rare|Yellow|Multiple bonuses + extra bonus if attribute requirement met|
|Legendary|Orange|Powerful build-specific buffs — the theme of the board|
|Glyph Socket|Red|Insert a Glyph to boost all nearby nodes|
|Board Attachment Gate|Special|+5 All Stats + attach next board|
**Rare Node Bonus Mechanic**
- Rare Nodes have a base bonus plus an extra bonus that unlocks when enough attribute nodes within range are allocated
- When requirement met the line turns green
- As you attach more boards the attribute requirements increase — harder to unlock on later boards
---
**Legendary Nodes**
- Most powerful nodes on any board
- Each board is themed around its Legendary Node
- Rotate boards to bring the Legendary Node as close as possible to your entry point — saves Paragon Points
---
**Glyphs (Quick Summary)**
- Insert into red Glyph Socket nodes
- Boosts all nodes within its radius
- Level up via Pit runs
- Max Glyph level is 100
- Upgrade to Legendary at level 45 using Gem Fragments
**Fast Paragon Leveling (Season 12 Meta)**
- Use Bloody Helltide Sigils after Level 60 — guarantees Ruthless Butcher spawns every wave which gives massive Paragon XP
- At Paragon 200 you can gain roughly 10 Paragon levels per 15 minutes using this method
- Run at 2–3 difficulty tiers lower than your current Torment — the sigil already applies a higher difficulty multiplier
