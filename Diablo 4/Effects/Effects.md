Here's a full breakdown of status effects in Diablo 4:
**Crowd Control (CC) — restrict enemy movement/actions:**
- **Stun** — enemy cannot move or act
- **Freeze** — enemy is encased in ice, cannot move or act
- **Chill** — slows enemy movement, builds up into Freeze
- **Slow** — reduces movement speed
- **Knockback** — physically pushes enemy away
- **Knockdown** — enemy is knocked to the ground briefly
- **Pull** — drags enemies toward a point
- **Immobilize** — roots enemy in place but they can still attack
- **Daze** — enemy cannot use special abilities but can still move
**Damage over Time (DoT):**
- **Burn** — fire damage over time
- **Poison** — nature/poison damage over time
- **Bleed** — physical damage over time
- **Shock** — lightning damage over time (less common as a DoT)
**Damage Amplifiers (debuffs that increase damage taken):**
- **Vulnerable** — 20% increased damage taken
- **Brittle** — increases chance of Critical Strike against the enemy
**Curses (Necromancer specific):**
- **Decrepify** — slows and weakens enemy
- **Iron Maiden** — reflects damage back to enemy
**Miscellaneous:**
- **Silence** — prevents skill use
- **Taunt** — forces enemy to attack a specific target
I'd recommend verifying the full list on wowhead or the Diablo 4 wiki as some of these may have been added, removed, or changed across seasons.
