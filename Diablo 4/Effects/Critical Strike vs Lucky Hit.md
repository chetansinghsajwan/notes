Here's a breakdown of both:
---
**Critical Strike Chance:**
- Every attack you make has a chance to be a **Critical Strike**
- A Critical Strike deals bonus damage — base is **50% increased damage**, which can be further increased by the **Critical Strike Damage** stat
- Critical Strike Chance is a percentage stat you build up through gear, skills, and passives
- It's one of the most fundamental damage stats in the game and almost every build wants to maximize it
---
**Lucky Hit:**
- Lucky Hit is a **secondary proc system** that is completely separate from Critical Strike
- Every skill has a **base Lucky Hit Chance** (varies per skill)
- When you have Lucky Hit effects on your gear or skills, those effects only trigger based on that skill's Lucky Hit Chance
- Think of it as a "chance to trigger a bonus effect" system
**Example:**
- A skill might have a 20% Lucky Hit Chance
- If you have an effect that says "Lucky Hit: chance to restore 10 Primary Resource"
- That effect will only have a 20% chance to trigger each time that skill hits
---
**How they differ:**
||Critical Strike|Lucky Hit|
|---|---|---|
|**What it does**|Increases damage|Triggers bonus effects|
|**Base chance**|Starts low, built up via gear|Per-skill, fixed base value|
|**Scaled by**|Critical Strike Chance stat|Individual skill's Lucky Hit Chance|
|**Primary use**|Damage optimization|Utility, resource, and additional effects|
Both are important but for different reasons — Critical Strike is a core damage multiplier, while Lucky Hit enables a whole layer of additional build mechanics.
