It is a bonus modifier that you can imprint on a gear that enhances your character’s abilities.
Aspects are categorized in **Offensive, Defensive, Utility, Resource, Mobility** types.
### ==How to get aspects==
==**Method 1 - Clearing Dungeons**==
- You unlock Aspects by completing certain end-game activities (like Nightmare Dungeons).
- These aspects are added into you **Codex of Power** and are unlocked permanently. Which means you can imprint them on gears unlimited times by paying a cost.
==**Method 2 - Extracting from items**==
- Legendary and Unique items come with aspects attached to them. These aspects are different from what you can find in the Codex of Power.
- You can extract these aspects from the items at the Occultist. But doing this destroys the gear.
- ==Question - Do you have to pay a price for this? probably yes.==
- These extracted aspects don’t go to the Codex of Power, instead to your inventory. These are only one time use. Which means after using them once. You will have to find them again from other gears.
- ==Fact - These aspects are usually better than you can find in the Codex of Power.==
