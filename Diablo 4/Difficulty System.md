The new difficulty system was introduced in Vessel of Hatred (Season 6) — Oct 2024.
**🟢 Normal**
- Slower-paced, for those looking for a lighter challenge
- Unlocked by default
- Base level XP and Gold (no bonus)
- Recommended for first-time players
---
**🟡 Hard**
- Unlocked by default
- +75% Gold and XP bonus
- Good for returning players who already have Renown/skills
---
**🟠 Expert**
- Unlocks after completing the prologue
- +125% Gold and XP bonus
- Recommended for players with some Legendary items
---
**🔴 Penitent**
- Unlocks after completing the base game campaign, or immediately for returning seasonal players
- +175% Gold and XP bonus
- Recommended for players with multiple tempered Legendary items
- Requires micromanagement in battle to survive
---
**💀 Torment 1**
- Requires Level 60 + complete Pit Tier 20
- Penalty: -250 Armor, -25% All Resistances
- +300% Gold and XP bonus
- Ancestral Legendary items start dropping
- Mythic Uniques can start dropping
- Infernal Hordes and Dark Citadel unlock
---
**💀💀 Torment 2**
- Requires Level 60 + complete Pit Tier 35
- Penalty: -500 Armor, -50% All Resistances
- Better Ancestral item rolls than T1
- Higher Mythic Unique drop chance
---
**💀💀💀 Torment 3**
- Requires Level 60 + complete Pit Tier 50
- Penalty: -750 Armor, -75% All Resistances
- +500% Gold and XP bonus
- A massive leap in difficulty from T2 — survival not guaranteed
---
**☠️ Torment 4**
- Requires Level 60 + complete Pit Tier 65
- Penalty: -1000 Armor, -100% All Resistances
- +600% Gold and XP bonus
- Best loot in the game
- Hardest content — most players never reach this
---
**General Notes**
- You can change difficulty at a statue found in every main city, marked by the Cathedral of Light symbol on the map
- Monster level is no longer displayed — enemies scale with your selected difficulty
- Smart keys like Infernal Hordes Compasses automatically match your current difficulty
