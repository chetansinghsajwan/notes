It is the process of restoring ==[[Items]]==.
- Can only be done at ==[[Blacksmith]]==
- Available from the start
- Costs only gold
==How to Repair==
- Visit any **Blacksmith** → Repair tab
- Cost increases every 10% durability lost and increases with Item Power
- Repairs item back to full 100/100 durability
==Repair Cost Factors==
|Factor|Effect on Cost|
|---|---|
|Item Rarity|Higher rarity = more expensive|
|Item Power|Higher power = more expensive|
|Durability Lost|More lost = more expensive|
