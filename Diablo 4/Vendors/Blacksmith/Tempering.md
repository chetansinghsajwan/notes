Tempering is the process of adding a bonus affix to an item on top of its existing affixes
- You can only do this at the ==[[Blacksmith]]==
- Unlocks from the start
- ==Only Rare and Legendary items can have 1 Tempering Affix applied to them==, with the value of the Affix being randomized from the displayed range
- Tempering binds the item to your account
### ==How Tempering Works==
- Go to ==Blacksmith → Temper tab==
- Choose an ==[[Items]]==, a ==[[Tempering]]== and a ==[[Tempering]].==
- Apply the tempering. This is also known as a roll. Each roll gives a random affix from that manual.
- ==Every temper roll has a small chance to give a Greater Affix.== GA tempering only works on Ancestral items (800+ item power) — tempering a non-Ancestral item will never roll a GA. Typically takes 5–10 rerolls to hit a GA temper — not as rare as most players think.
- ==Each item has 3 Tempering charges and +1 charge for each greater affix the item has== — shown as a durability bar on the item. If you use all attempts the item becomes bricked — the temper slot is locked permanently with whatever affix it last rolled.
- ==You can use Scrolls of Restoration to reset temper attempts on an item== — restores durability bar back to full. Scrolls of Restoration drop from Torment difficulty content — Helltide, Nightmare Dungeons, Infernal Hordes.
### ==Tempering Manuals and Recipies==
- ==The Tempering system makes use of items called Tempering Manuals.== Use a manual to learn the ==Tempering Recipe== permanently. There is no limit to how many times players can reuse a recipe once they've learned it.
- ==Each Manual teaches a Recipe that contains between 3 to 5 different Tempering Affixes.==
- ==Tempering Manuals are class-bound== (e.g., only Rogues will find Rogue Temper Manuals) when being discovered and tempering your equipment with another class' Tempering Manual will cause the tempering to be inactive on other classes. You cannot temper your equipment with, say, Cold Damage from a Sorcerer and use it on a Rogue.
- ==Tempering Manuals can drop from most content in-game.== They have a high chance of finding them from completing events and defeating monsters in [Helltides](https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/helltide-guide), defeating world bosses, completing dungeons, [Nightmare Dungeons](https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/nightmare-dungeons), the [Pit of Artificers](https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/pit-guide), and other endgame activities.
