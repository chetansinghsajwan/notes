It is an endgame system at the Blacksmith that improves the quality of affixes on your gear.
- Can only be done at ==[[Blacksmith]]==.
- Unlocks at ==character level 60==.
### ==How masterworking works:==
- Every item has 25 ranks of quality.
- Each upgrade increases Quality by a random amount between 2 and 5 ranks.
- Each +1 Quality adds +1% to all affix values AND +1% to base stats (Weapon Damage, Armor, Resistances)
- At Quality 25 you unlock a final Capstone Bonus upgrade
### ==Capstone Bonus (Quality 25)==
- Once item reaches Quality 25 you perform one final upgrade — the Capstone Bonus which increases one random affix by +50%
- Capstone Bonus stacks on top of Greater Affix bonus — always aim to stack both
- Capstone Bonus can be rerolled using Neathiron without resetting the entire Quality rank — grind for perfect affix
### ==Resetting Masterwork==
- Can reset at any time at the Blacksmith
- Reset costs: 30 Iron Chunks/Rawhide + 20 Veiled Crystals + 5 Forgotten Souls + 5,000,000 Gold
- Resets item back to rank zero — no partial resets available
- Materials are NOT fully refunded — only partial refund
- Only reset if Capstone Bonus landed on wrong affix
### ==Pro Tips==
- Never Masterwork before finishing tempering — waste of materials if you brick the temper
- Always target the Capstone Bonus landing on your most important affix
- Farm Nightmare Dungeons with Treasure Breach sigil affix — by far the best Obducite source in Season 12 [Maxroll.gg](https://maxroll.gg/d4/resources/difficulty-overview)
- Save Neathiron exclusively for high rank upgrades and Capstone rerolls
- Getting Capstone Bonus on a Greater Affix is the ultimate goal — massive power spike
- Reset only when absolutely necessary — gold and material cost is extremely high
