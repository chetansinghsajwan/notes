==What is Forge Equipment?==
- A Blacksmith feature that lets you craft item Caches using crafting materials
- Useful for targeting specific item types or getting Mythic Uniques
- Available once you've progressed enough to have excess materials
==What You Can Craft==
==1. Blacksmith Cache==
- Contains one Legendary item or higher quality
- Good for targeting a specific item slot when drops aren't cooperating
==2. Mythic Unique==
- Craft a random Mythic Unique using 2x Resplendent Sparks
- Cannot choose which Mythic Unique you get — completely random
- Best used when you have duplicate/unwanted Mythic Uniques to convert
==How to Get Resplendent Sparks==
|Source|Notes|
|---|---|
|Salvage unwanted Mythic Uniques|Each salvage gives 1 Resplendent Spark|
|Seasonal Journey rewards|Occasional reward from completing chapters|
|Uber Boss kills|Rare drop from endgame boss kills|
**Key Rules**
- Need **2 Resplendent Sparks** per Mythic Unique craft
- Cannot target a specific Mythic Unique — purely random
- Resplendent Sparks are extremely rare — don't waste them
- Seasonal Resplendent Sparks do not carry over to next season
---
**Pro Tips**
- Only craft Mythic Uniques when you have enough Sparks AND your current Mythic is useless for your build
- If you get duplicate Mythic Uniques, salvage the worse one for Sparks
- Don't craft blindly — check which Mythic Uniques are best for your class/build first
- Sparks reset each season — use them before season ends
