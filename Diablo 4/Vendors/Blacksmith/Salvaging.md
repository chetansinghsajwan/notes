It is the process of breaking down unwanted items into crafting materials.
- This can only be done at the ==[[Blacksmith]]==
- Permanently destroys the item — cannot be undone
- Available from the start
- Items marked as favorite cannot be salvaged
- Salvaging Legendaries automatically saves their Aspect to your Codex of Power — or upgrades rank if you already have it
- Salvaging any item unlocks its Transmog appearance at the Wardrobe permanently
- Gems socketed in items are returned to inventory
- Salvaging Tempered or Masterworked gear recoups some of the materials spent on it

### ==Auto Salvage==
It is a system that automatically salvages lower rarity items and converts them directly into crafting materials
- No manual intervention needed — materials go straight to your stash
- Materials are also auto-picked up — you never see the items drop at all
- After ==Torment 1== all Normal, Magic, and Rare items are auto salvaged — only Legendary and Unique items drop
### ==Pro Tips==
- Always salvage before selling — materials are far more valuable than gold
- Hover over item to check if it shows "unlocks new look on salvage" — if yes, always salvage at least once for the Transmog
- Ancestral Legendaries give 10x materials — prioritize salvaging these when replacing gear
- Keep Forgotten Souls carefully — they're needed for Occultist enchanting on high tier items
- Use Favorite mark on items you want to keep — prevents accidental bulk salvage
