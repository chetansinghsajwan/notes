==What is Enchanting?==
- Rerolling one existing affix on an item to get a different one
- Done at the ==Occultist==
- Unlocks from the start
- You can only enchant [[Affix]]. You can’t enchant a tempered affix and enchanting a [[Affix]] replaces it with a normal affix. You can’t get a greater affix through enchanting.
- Enchanting costs ==Gold (increases per attempt), Veiled Crystals, sometimes Forgotten Souls for high tier items==.
==How Enchanting Works==
- Select item at Occultist → Enchant tab
- Choose which affix to reroll
- Each reroll shows 2 options — pick one or keep the original
- Cost increases significantly with each reroll attempt on the same item
- No limit on attempts — but cost keeps rising
