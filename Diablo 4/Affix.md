In diablo 4 an affix is just a feature.
==For example,==
- +10% attack speed
- 450 poison resistance
Note that it is different from the base stat. For example, 400 damage from sword or 300 resistance from armor.
==Number of Affixes==
|   |   |
|---|---|
|Item Rarity|# of Affix|
|Common|0|
|Magic|1-2|
|Rare|3-4|
|Legendary|3-4|
|Unique|Fixed (preset)|
|Mythic Unique|Fixed (preset)|
### ==Greater Affix==
A greater affix is an affix with maximum possible power + 50% bonus on top.
- You can find greater affixes from loot items, or you can temper an item to put a greater affix on it.
