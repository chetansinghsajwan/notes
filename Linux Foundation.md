The **Linux Foundation** (**LF**) is a [non-profit organization](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Non-profit_organization) established in 2000 to support [Linux](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Linux) development and [open-source software](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Open-source_software) projects.
