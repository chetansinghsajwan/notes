A real-time digital payment system built by **NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India)**, launched in April 2016, under RBI oversight. It links multiple bank accounts into a single mobile app for instant 24/7 transfers.
### Key Facts
|Parameter|Detail|
|---|---|
|Full Form|Unified Payments Interface|
|Launched|April 2016|
|Developed by|NPCI (under RBI)|
|Transaction limit|₹1 lakh per transaction (₹2 lakh for some categories)|
|Availability|24×7, 365 days|
|Banks on UPI|691+ (as of Jan 2026)|
|Security (from Apr 2026)|Mandatory 2FA — biometrics/PIN + OTP|
---
### How it Works
- You get a **VPA (Virtual Payment Address)** like `name@bank` — no need to share account/IFSC
- Payment goes: Your app → NPCI switch → Recipient's bank — all in **seconds**

### Scale (2026)
- Processed **16.6 billion transactions** in Feb 2026, worth ~₹22.8 lakh crore (~$274B) — a 34% jump YoY.
- Handles ~**49% of global real-time payments** (2024).
---
### Where UPI Works Internationally
Countries accepting UPI: **Bhutan, France, Mauritius, Nepal, Singapore, Sri Lanka, UAE.**
---
### Why It Matters for India
- Enabled financial inclusion — street vendors and MSMEs now use UPI transaction history to access formal credit from banks.
- Integrated with **JAM Trinity** (Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile) for direct benefit transfers like PM-KISAN.
