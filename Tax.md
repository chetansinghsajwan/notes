- **Tax** is a **mandatory payment** collected by the government from individuals and businesses.
- It is **not optional** and **not a fee** for a specific service.
- Used to fund **public goods**: infrastructure, healthcare, defense, education, administration.
**Core idea**
- Tax is how a society **collectively pays** for things no single person can efficiently fund alone.
**Who pays tax**
- **Individuals** → income tax, indirect taxes.
- **Businesses** → corporate tax, GST.
- **Consumers** → indirect taxes embedded in prices.
**Main tax types (conceptual)**
- **Direct tax**
	- Paid **directly** to the government.
	- Burden cannot be shifted.
	- Example: income tax.
- **Indirect tax**
	- Paid **indirectly** through purchases.
	- Burden is shifted to the consumer.
	- Example: GST.
**Why governments tax**
- Run the government.
- Redistribute wealth.
- Influence behavior (sin tax, subsidies).
- Control inflation and economic activity.
**Key tax terms**
- **Taxable income**: income on which tax is calculated.
- **Tax rate**: percentage charged.
- **Tax slab**: income range with a specific rate.
- **Deduction**: amount reduced before tax calculation.
- **Exemption**: income not taxed at all.
- **TDS**: tax deducted at source before you receive income.
**Simple example**
- You earn **₹6,00,000/year**.
- After deductions, taxable income becomes **₹5,00,000**.
- Tax is calculated on ₹5,00,000, not ₹6,00,000.
**Important property**
- Tax is enforced by law.
- Non-payment can lead to **penalties or prosecution**.
**One-line summary**
- **Tax = compulsory contribution to run a country and its systems.**
