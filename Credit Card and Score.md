# Credit History
It accounts for around 15% of the credit score.
# Credit Mix
It accounts for around 10% of the credit score.
It refers the various accounts you have such as credit cards, mortgages, car loans, or student loans.
# Effects of closing a credit card
Closing a credit card affects you credit score in the following ways:
- **Credit Utilization**
    
	It increases you credit utilization as because your total limit is reduced.
    
- **Credit History**
    
	Reduces the average age of your accounts. The average age your accounts makes up to around 15% of credit score. Closing you oldest credit card affects the most.
    
- **Credit Mix**
    
	Reduces the scope of credit mix. Credit mix accounts for around 10% of credit score.
